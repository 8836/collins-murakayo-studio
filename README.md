# Collins Murakayo – Enterprise Portfolio Website

## Objective

Build a world-class, enterprise-grade portfolio website for **Collins Murakayo**, showcasing his work as a **Graphic Designer, Brand Identity Designer, Creative Strategist, Data Analyst, and Business Operations Professional**.

The portfolio should position Collins as a designer capable of working with organizations such as Microsoft, Google, Adobe, Airbnb, Stripe, Notion, IBM, Deloitte, McKinsey, and leading international creative agencies.

This is **not** a template portfolio.

It should feel like an award-winning digital experience that communicates confidence, precision, craftsmanship, and strategic thinking.

Every design decision should reinforce professionalism and exceptional attention to detail.

The portfolio should leave visitors with the impression that they are viewing the work of a senior multidisciplinary designer.

Automatically choose the strongest visual as the project cover image.
